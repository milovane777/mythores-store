Mythores is a pet collecting game with no server behind it. No account, no ads,
no purchases. Every friend and every record lives on this phone alone.

- They grow into how you raise them
Feed, play, wash, brush, tidy up, and put to bed.
Keep up with all six and your friend grows bright-eyed and stands tall.
Fall behind and their coat dulls, their head drops, their smile flattens.
The same species from the same egg becomes a different animal in someone
else's hands.

- Forty-eight species, six tiers
Pandas, capybaras, sea otters, hummingbirds, sloths, jellyfish, bats —
and six dragons, six dinosaurs, a phoenix, a unicorn and a nine-tailed fox.
Each dragon and creature of myth is four times rarer than an animal.
Common through Mythic, plus a one-in-4096 shiny at every tier.
Mythic carries an issue number, a hundred to a device and no more.
Every probability is published inside the app.

- Four bands of the world
Eggs only hatch in the longitude band your phone is in.
Friends from the other three can only arrive as a gift from someone who
lives there. The last part of the codex cannot be finished alone.

- Seasons
Each quarter favours a different habitat.
A friend born in a past season only ever comes from someone who was there.

- Gifting
A QR code in the same room, or a code sent by message to the other side of
the world. Every pet is protected by its device's signature, and the card
remembers everyone who has held it.
A gift is a gift: nothing in the app puts a value on a pet.

- Dress up
Scenes, hats, scarves and things for the floor, bought with coins you earn.
A friend you give away keeps the outfit they were wearing.

- Backups
Your friends and this device's key go into one passphrase-locked file.
Lose the phone and that one file brings all of it back.
The passphrase never leaves the device; forget it and we cannot open it either.

- Showing, and checking
One shelf code shows a friend everything you have. It is not a gift — nobody
gains a pet from it.
Once a season you can sign what condition your friend is in today. A run of
seasons in a row means somebody was there for all of it.
Someone who is not the owner can sign the guestbook to say they saw this one,
and a friend signed in all four bands has genuinely been around the world.
The app keeps a tally of what it has checked itself, so a card can say which
of the ones you have seen this is.

- What this app does not have
No server, account, sign-in, ads, purchases, chat, or data collection.
A month with nobody visiting and a friend goes. The last week is said
plainly on the card and in the list, and a notification comes — one visit
inside it is enough. Afterwards the card and everything on it stay exactly as
they are.
